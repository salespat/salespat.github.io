Capabilities

Built to absorb the work that's frequent, necessary, and low-leverage.

SalesPat targets the tasks that matter to execution quality but never required a senior architect or engineer to rebuild them by hand. It does the busywork; you keep the judgment.

Preparation & synthesis

Consolidates account context, prior interactions, and meeting objectives into structured, ready-to-use prep — no manual digging.

Standard technical outputs

Accelerates routine follow-ups, recaps, repeatable technical messaging, and first-draft artifacts with consistent quality.

Execution support at scale

Offloads repetitive coordination and documentation across the motion so senior technical capacity isn't consumed by overhead.

Operating model

Automation handles repetition. Experts handle judgment.

SalesPat is a control layer for repeatable work — not a replacement for technical talent. It absorbs standardized, process-heavy tasks and returns time to the experts who create disproportionate customer value.

That boundary is deliberate. Enterprise teams need efficiency without diluting technical credibility, so humans keep ownership of the decisions, conversations, and recommendations that depend on context and experience.

Replace manual repetition — not technical expertise. The goal is more strategic bandwidth for pre-sales, never commoditizing the roles that win trust.
